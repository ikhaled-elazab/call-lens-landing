## Section: How It Works
- Section eyebrow: How It Works
- Section headline: From Recording to Recommendation in Minutes

1. **Ingest** — Call Lens connects to your dialer, contact center, or conferencing tool and ingests every recorded call automatically.
2. **Transcribe & Analyze** — AI transcribes each call and analyzes language, tone, and structure against your defined guidelines.
3. **Score** — Every call receives a guideline compliance score plus flagged metrics (talk ratio, sentiment, key moments).
4. **Extract Insights** — Objections, risks, and coaching-relevant moments are tagged and surfaced automatically.
5. **Deliver Coaching** — Managers receive a ready-to-use coaching recommendation, linked to the exact moment in the call.
