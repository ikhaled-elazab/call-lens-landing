# Brand Brief — Call Lens (v0.1)

## Product
**Call Lens** — a Conversation Intelligence platform that analyzes sales and support calls, extracts metrics and insights against organization-defined guidelines, and delivers coaching recommendations to operations managers.

## Tone / Voice
Confident, data-forward, outcome-oriented. Never hyperbolic. Speaks like a analytics/ops peer, not a hype-driven startup. Plain language first, technical precision second.

## Primary Audience
- **Operations Managers / Team Leads** — need to know which reps need coaching and why, backed by evidence from real calls.
- **Sales & Support Leaders** — need aggregate visibility into guideline compliance and call quality trends.

## Pain Points Addressed
1. Can't review 100% of calls manually — insights are missed.
2. No consistent way to score calls against internal guidelines/scripts.
3. Coaching is reactive and anecdotal, not data-driven.
4. Hard to prove ROI of coaching interventions.

## Approved Terminology
- "Conversation Intelligence" (primary category term)
- "Guideline-based scoring" (not "compliance policing")
- "Coaching recommendations" (not "grades" or "report cards")
- "Operations managers" as primary persona label

## Forbidden / Avoid
- "Spy on your reps" / surveillance framing
- Unverified stats or fabricated customer logos
- Overpromising ("100% accurate", "replaces managers")

## Design Direction
Navy (#0B1E39) + Teal accent (#14B8A6), clean sans-serif (Inter), professional B2B SaaS aesthetic — trustworthy, data-forward, not playful.
