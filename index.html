Computed, not guessed

A model would move these between runs. These don't move.

Word-level timings make silence, overlap and response speed measurable, not estimated. The twenty-two measures Call Lens computes are calculated the same way every time — reproducible, and defensible in a performance review. A rep can't argue with a number that doesn't change when you rerun it.

  • When a measure can't be taken, the call names the reason and is excluded from averages — never counted as zero.
  • Citations are checked against the real transcript before they're stored. A claim pointing at a second that doesn't exist is refused, not shown.
  • Re-running a call adds a version. It never overwrites what a decision was already made on.

On every call, automatically

The inventory

Capture

  • An ingestion API for your dialler or contact-centre platform, or browser upload — MP3, M4A, AAC, WAV, WebM, OGG, FLAC, up to 200MB or 60 minutes.
  • Idempotent submission: a retrying integration cannot create the same call twice.
  • Eleven named processing stages, each visible as pending, running, succeeded, failed or skipped. When one stops, the call says which and why.
  • Audio normalised before analysis; a waveform rendered for the whole call; the player jumps straight to any cited second.

Speech and speakers

  • Word-level timings — what makes silence, overlap and response speed measurable rather than estimated.
  • United States English and Arabic dialects — Egyptian, Saudi and Emirati — handled natively, not flattened into generic Arabic. See the block below.
  • True dual-channel speaker separation. The employee is identified by name — never "speaker 1".
  • One-click correction when the wrong speaker was marked; every measure recomputes.

Coaching

  • Seven moment types: coachable, risk, missed close, no next step agreed, strong discovery, price pushback, competitor mentioned.
  • Each moment carries a "try instead" line — a concrete alternative sentence, not a grade.
  • Risks typed — churn, compliance, competitor, pricing, expectations — and graded low, medium or high.
  • One org-wide feed, filterable by rep, date range, moment type and severity, with totals before you open anything.

Scoring

  • Your own scorecard templates — your criteria, your weights, not a generic one.
  • Scored automatically against measured behaviour, with criterion-level evidence.
  • Honest partial scoring: it reports how many criteria could be evaluated and withholds a total when none could.
  • Changing a scorecard never rewrites scores already given.

The coverage argument

The same hours. Five times the calls.

A call reviewed by ear runs in real time. A call reviewed in Call Lens is a summary, five judgments and the flagged moments, each with a timecode: read the page, play the two seconds that matter.

Eight calls a person a month, reviewed by ear, or forty, reviewed this way — the same management time. Run it on your own numbers: 5× coverage, roughly the same hours. Or hold coverage flat and take the hours back instead.

  • One org-wide coaching feed, filterable by rep, date, moment type and severity — totals and high-severity counts before you open anything.
  • A signed webhook to your CRM as each call completes, with delivery history and a readiness check before you switch it on.
  • CSV export of any filtered call list.

Trust

What isolation actually means here

Isolation

  • Tenant isolation is enforced by the database itself on every query, not by application code.
  • The application's own database account was never granted the ability to bypass it.
  • Outside an authorised session, a query returns nothing at all — not someone else's rows.

Recordings and retention

  • Recording links expire on a window you configure.
  • Every authorisation and every refusal is logged — who asked, which call, from which address.
  • Retention set on your contract and visible in the product. Deletion by period, or call by call.
  • Subject erasure produces a receipt of what was removed — and names anything that couldn't be erased, rather than glossing over it.
